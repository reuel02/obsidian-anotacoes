|Option|Primary use case|When to reach for it|Key limitation|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Client script**|Lightweight UI logic that reacts to a user's action in real time|Validating or formatting a field, or showing and hiding controls, as someone fills out a form|Tied to one form and event. Not reusable as a visual component|
|**PCF control**|A reusable, richer input or visualization control|The same specialized control is needed across multiple forms or apps|More development and packaging effort than a script|
|**Code app**|A custom business web app built code-first|A developer needs full control over UI and logic while staying on the managed platform|Requires IDE-based development skills and tooling, not a low-code designer|
|**Generative page**|Fast, natural-language scaffolding of a model-driven app page|You want a working page quickly from a description, then refine it|Generated output still needs review. Some capabilities remain preview|
